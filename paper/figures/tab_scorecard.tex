C2 & Typing, units          & 3 & Partial & Signals and settings declare \texttt{type}, \texttt{unit} and \texttt{limits} (range or enum), validated before vendor code runs. C3 & Structured errors      & 3 & Partial & Three of four sub-tests pass: 13 declared codes, a message, and a populated structured context (\texttt{LimitViolation} carries the violated bound, \texttt{InvalidValue} the expected type, \texttt{NotLeased} the holder and reason). C4 & Open API access        & 6 & Pass    & Apache 2.0, no royalties, integrations publishable and reusable, documentation free. Caveat: a device behind a proprietary SDK inherits that vendor's terms; the protocol cannot launder them. \\
C5 & Result retrieval       & 3 & Partial & Headless and execution-linked: the job snapshot returns the result with its id, state, outcome, start and finish times, and the owning method and project. One sub-test misses: \texttt{result} is untyped and actions declare no \texttt{returns} schema, so result structure and units are per-driver convention rather than part of the contract. \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{5}{@{}l}{\textit{Important}}\\
C6  & Runtime self-description & 2 & Pass    & \texttt{device/describe} with three tiers and \texttt{select}; a generic client enumerates the surface with no prior knowledge. \\
C7  & Observable state         & 2 & Pass    & \texttt{jobs/status} never blocks on completion; progress, signals, safety and operator events are pushed. \\
C8  & Reference client         & 2 & Pass    & Python SDK, CLI, MCP bridge, worked examples and tested recipes. \\
C9  & Concurrent access        & 2 & Partial & Atomic \texttt{session/acquire} with TTL, visible holder, renewal and reconnect. No role-gated administrative clear. \\
C10 & Device/readiness state   & 2 & Pass    & Named states with machine-readable recovery through verified \texttt{safety/reset}. \\
C11 & Result durability        & 2 & Partial & Run records persist on disk with stable ids; the device job table is in memory, so a server restart loses job-level results. \\
C12 & Instrument self-state    & 2 & \textbf{Fail} & The protocol defines no place for calibration state, qualification, usage counters or out-of-spec flags. A driver may expose them as signals, but nothing names or requires them, so no two devices agree. \\
C13 & Versioning discipline    & 2 & Partial & \texttt{PROTOCOL\_VERSION} is negotiated at \texttt{initialize} and echoed in \texttt{describe}. No deprecation policy and no support commitment; pre-1.0 states that breaking changes are expected. \\
C14 & Simulation mode          & 3 & Partial & Every package ships a drop-in twin on the same interface. No configurable fault injection, which the criterion requires. \\
C15 & Control-loss observability & 3 & \textbf{Pass} & The watchdog is armed while the lease is held; silence past the declared window is itself the signal. The device reports and fails safe without the agent or the network, and the window is declared per device rather than imposed. \\
C16 & Error recovery taxonomy  & 2 & Partial & Execution effect (\texttt{outcome: unknown}) and post-error state are machine-readable. Transience, recovery class and a physical-motion flag are not declared fields. \\
C17 & Conformance evidence     & 2 & Partial & Four behavioural levels and a suite that exercises them. No version-tied evidence, and the device-model dimension is weak, which caps this at Partial by the paper's own rule. \\
C18 & OS independence          & 2 & Pass    & Pure Python over a neutral network service; the node service installs on Linux, macOS and Windows. \\
