\caption{Retrieval under growth, 200 trials per point. A query states a task and a bay;
a trial succeeds when the first result is an instrument of the class that performs the
task, in the requested bay. Free-text ranking alone degrades from 86\% to 59\% as the
laboratory grows, reproducing the selection-accuracy decay reported for large tool
sets~\cite{anthropic-advanced-tool-use}. The structured filters that
\texttt{directory/search} already accepts, and that the bridge issues, hold accuracy at
99.5--100\% across three orders of magnitude. The stability comes from the protocol's
structure rather than from the ranker.}
